Strike Commander MVRS manoeuvres

This document describes what each AI manoeuvre does in the original game: when it is chosen, how it is set up, what it commands phase by phase and when it ends. Each card ends with a list of points to observe in the DebugStrike test tool, to compare the port with the original.

All values come from the score, apply and tick functions read in seg002, seg003 and seg008. Angles are in degrees, distances in metres, speeds in the unit of the JDYN fields, durations in seconds. Stick: 16 = full deflection.

Common framework: when a manoeuvre runs

A manoeuvre is a behaviour: once applied, it is pushed (Behavior_PushRunning_756A4) and keeps control of the aircraft until it pops itself (Behavior_PopFinished_75612) or is abandoned. In the meantime, its tick is called at every AI tick.

flowchart TD
  T["AI_TopLevelThink (every tick)"] --> A["Threat alert?
abandons and applies ID 4 (3 s)"]
  T --> R["Nothing running: reflexes
ID 15 stall, ID 14 ground, collision"]
  T --> M["Missile evasion / reaction to an attacker"]
  T --> G["Profile GOAL"]
  G --> S["AI_BehaviorStateMachine
(air target, reaction ≤ 1)"]
  S --> B{"AI_BehaviorSelector:
shot or firing solution?"}
  B -- yes --> F["Pursuit and fire,
running behaviour abandoned"]
  B -- no --> C{"Behaviour running?"}
  C -- yes --> K["its tick"]
  C -- no --> TO["MVRS tournament
score + PROF weight ± 1"]
  TO --> AP["Winner applied
(pushed, 2 s timer by default)"]
  • The tournament only runs when there is an air target but no firing solution. As soon as AI_BehaviorSelector finds a positive shot quality, the running manoeuvre is abandoned.
  • Candidates: the 8 fixed nodes (ID 20, 14, 15, 16, 21, 7, 19, 4) and the identifiers of the MVRS chunk (25 at most). Rating = node score + weight from the file ± 1 at random. A score of 0 excludes the candidate, whatever its weight. On a tie, the first one keeps its place.
  • The tournament passes each node a context: the air target and a 2 s timer. An apply that does not set its own timer keeps these 2 s (ID 2, ID 4 outside an alert).
  • “Uses” = number of times the manoeuvre has already been applied (node+2). Several scores subtract it: the AI varies its manoeuvres.
  • Every apply switches off the autopilot (JDYN+0x68 = 0xFF).

Vocabulary of scores and ticks

Scores read a context computed once per frame (MVRS_BuildCombatContext_E5A4). The names below are this document's; the original's global variable is in parentheses.

Nose angle
Angle between my nose and the direction of the target: 0 = dead ahead, 180 = dead astern (word_72095).
Pointing
Angle between the target's velocity and the target → me line: 0 = the target is heading straight at me, 180 = it is moving away (word_72093 = 180 − word_72097).
Aspect
Angle between the me → target line and the target's velocity (word_72097): 0 = it has its back to me.
Future angle
Angle between my nose and the target's relative position in 3 s (word_720DD).
Future altitude difference
Target's altitude minus mine, both extrapolated to 3 s (dword_720D9).
d
Distance to the target (dword_7209B). Usual threshold: 1800 m (dword_7201C).
Close
d < 3 × the component of the target's velocity along the line of sight.
Target behind me
Nose angle ≥ 100 and (future angle ≤ 80, or pointing < 50 and close) (byte_720E0).
Head-on
Nose angle ≤ 60, pointing < 60 and close (byte_720E1).
Aircraft
The AI entity is of type 1, an aircraft (byte_720DF). True for all airborne AIs.
Fighter
Target's model class > 6 (byte_720C3 > byte_72038).
Floor
Terrain altitude + 200 m (dword_7203D, entity+0xE5 = 200 m).
Too low
Altitude < terrain + 4 × 200 = terrain + 800 m (AI_Sensor_TooLow_56E5).
Min manoeuvre speed
Min speed JDYN+0x82, raised towards cruise when the nose is high (dword_72039).
Too slow
Stalled, or indicated speed ≤ min manoeuvre speed (AI_Sensor_TooSlow_564A).
Cruise, min, max
JDYN+0x84, +0x82, +0x80. “Full throttle” = notch 10 written directly; “cruise throttle” = throttle law towards cruise speed.
Attitude(a, z)
Commands pitch towards a; “reached” when the error is below the dead zone z (AI_PitchToAngleCmd_7E18).
Roll(r, z)
Same for bank (AI_RollToAngleCmd_8104).
Guidance(D)
Steering law towards a direction D (AI_GuidanceSolution_Major), with altitude protections. Returns “aligned” when the heading and elevation errors are zero.

ID 1 · tag 0x2B4

Reacquisition by heading segments

  • Trigger tournament
  • Duration 4 segments
  • Throttle full (segments)
  • Functions MVRS_ID1_*

Score (base 5)

Zero if: no target, target not a fighter, pointing > 20, too slow, too low, or aircraft with the target on my six. Then − uses/2.

  • Nose angle > 90 (target on the side or behind): +2, and another +2 if nose angle > 135 and d < 1800.
  • Otherwise: −3 (target ahead), −3 if d > 1800.
  • Aircraft: − FL/2. Clamped to 0…9.

Setup

The first segment aims at my nose rotated by 30° towards the target's side, level. A segment is an ID 20 node of 1 s. Node timer: 4 (counts the segments).

Sequence

  1. S
    Each segment: guidance towards its direction, full throttle, until 1 s or alignment.
  2. T
    At the end of a segment, the ID 1 tick subtracts 1 from the timer, measures the heading error towards the target, then starts a new segment: my current nose rotated by −60°, level, 1 s.

End

Heading error towards the target < 60°, no more target, or timer at 0 (4 segments in all).

Bug in the original: the alternation test (test ax, 0) is always false. All the following segments turn by −60°. If the target is on the “+” side, the 2nd segment heads off away from the target.

To check in the tool

  • First segment: heading turning by about 30° towards the target, level, full throttle.
  • Following segments: −60° relative to the nose at that moment, always to the same side.
  • At most 4 segments; ends as soon as the target is within 60° in heading.
ID 2 · tag 0x28C

Barrel-roll break

  • Trigger tournament
  • Duration 2 s
  • Throttle manoeuvre speed
  • Functions MVRS_ID2_*

Score (base 5)

Zero if: no target, target not a fighter, pointing > 20, too slow, too low, or if the entity is an aircraft. An AI aircraft therefore never chooses this manoeuvre in the tournament.

  • − uses/2. Nose angle > 135, pointing < 30 and d < 1800: +7.
  • Otherwise: −3 if target ahead, −3 if d > 1800.
  • Indicated speed < min: −6. SM skill roll succeeded: +3.

Setup

The roll direction follows the lateral stick at that moment: > 3 → + side, < −3 → − side, otherwise drawn at random.

Sequence (every tick)

  1. R
    Lateral stick fully over (±16) to the chosen side, continuously: the aircraft rolls non-stop.
  2. P
    Pitch stick pulled fully back (16, limited by the pilot) when the nose is above the horizon on its dominant axis, otherwise pushed halfway (−8).
  3. V
    Speed: AI_ManeuverSpeedCmd_ED1E with the target's speed.

End

2 s timer elapsed, or no more target.

Peculiarity: the node's reference vector (node+0x26, +0x2A) is zeroed at construction and never written. The “pull or push” choice therefore depends only on the aircraft's attitude.

To check in the tool

  • In the tournament, an AI aircraft must never choose ID 2.
  • When forced: continuous roll to the same side for 2 s, pitch stick at +16 or −8.
ID 3 · tag 0x278

Energy manoeuvre

  • Trigger tournament
  • Duration 4 s
  • Throttle cruise
  • Functions MVRS_ID3_*

Score (base 5)

Zero if: no target, target not a fighter, pointing > 20. No usage penalty.

  • Nose angle < 90 (target ahead): −3.
  • Nose angle > 135 and d ≤ 1800: +3. d > 1800: −3.
  • Aircraft: − FL/4.

Sequence

Base slope: p = 5 + 40 × (FL/16)² degrees (5° at FL 0, 45° at FL 16).

  1. 0
    Choice. Too slow and not too low → 2 (dive). Too slow and too low → 1 (jinks). Too low only → 4 (climb). Otherwise at random: 1 chance in 4 → 2, 1 in 4 → 4, 2 in 4 → 1.
  2. 1
    Jink: ID 20 segment of 1 s towards my nose rotated by 32° or 64° (at random), the side alternating from one time to the next; timer −1 s; back to 0.
  3. 2
    Cruise throttle, roll towards 180° (zone 15) then pull fully. Moves to 3 as soon as the attitude is ≥ −30°, i.e. immediately in practice.
  4. 3
    Dive: attitude −p (zone 10) until the indicated speed reaches cruise or the aircraft is too low; then attitude +5 and back to 0.
  5. 4
    Wings level (zone 15) then pull fully. Moves to 5 as soon as the attitude is ≤ 30°, so usually immediately.
  6. 5
    Climb: attitude +p (zone 10) while the aircraft is below terrain + 800 m and not too slow; otherwise attitude −5 and back to 0.
  7. 6
    Timer elapsed: cruise throttle, attitude 0 (zone 5), then end.

To check in the tool

  • Dive: attitude −p held until cruise speed, then pull back up to +5°.
  • Climb: only below terrain + 800 m; stops above.
  • Jinks: heading changes of 32° or 64°, alternating side, each jink takes 1 s off.
  • Ends after about 4 s, on a levelling-off.
ID 4 · tag 0x264 · fixed node +0xBD

Defensive turn

  • Trigger threat alert, also in the tournament
  • Duration 3 s (alert) / 2 s (tournament), +2 s grace
  • Throttle full

Triggered by an alert

AI_TopLevelThink: if AI_IncomingThreatWarning answers, the running behaviour is abandoned and the ID 4 node is applied directly, with the threat as target and a 3 s timer.

Tournament score (base 5)

Zero if: no target, target not a fighter, pointing > 20, too slow, too low, or aircraft with the target on my six. No usage penalty.

  • Target ahead (nose angle < 35): +4 if future angle > 135 and pointing < 45 (we are about to cross), otherwise −10.
  • Nose angle > 90: +2, and +2 if nose angle > 135 and d < 1800.
  • Otherwise: −2 (target ahead), −3 if d > 3600.
  • Indicated speed < min: −8; otherwise < cruise: −2.

Setup

Turn side = side of the target relative to my nose. The initial horizontal heading is stored.

Sequence (every tick)

  1. 1
    Full throttle. Desired bank: 90°, or 60° if too low.
  2. 2
    Roll towards that bank (zone 30). Once within 30°: roll (zone 5) and stick pulled fully back.
  3. 3
    When the heading has turned by 90° or more, or the timer has elapsed: attitude +10 (zone 10).

End

Attitude +10 reached, or timer below −2 s.

To check in the tool

  • Full throttle, bank rising to 90° (60° below terrain + 800 m) towards the target's side.
  • Stick pulled fully back only once the bank is within 30° of the setpoint.
  • Ends around 90° of heading or at the end of the timer, after pulling up to +10°.
ID 5 · tag 0x250

Vertical reversal

  • Trigger tournament
  • Duration 5 s, then 10 s for each phase
  • Throttle max (phases 2 to 4), cruise afterwards

Score (base 3)

Zero if: no target, too slow, target on my six, indicated speed < cruise, or entity not an aircraft. Then − 2 × uses. Starting phase: 3.

  • Future altitude difference < −2500: −7; ≥ +1250: +2.
  • Target ahead (nose angle < 35): +4 if future angle > 145 and pointing < 45, otherwise −10.
  • Nose angle > 90 and pointing < 45: +7 if d < 3600, otherwise −2.
  • My speed < half of the target's: −4; < the target's: −2; > double: +2.
  • Agility JDYN+0x8B = a: +(3 + (a−2)×3/2) if a ≥ 2, otherwise −(3 + (2−a)×3/2).
  • Too low: +4. Otherwise, above 25,000 m: −6 and start in phase 1.

Sequence

  1. 1
    Above 22,500 m: dive −30° (zone 10). Otherwise phase 2.
  2. 2
    Speed recovery: max throttle, attitude −30° (zone 5), or +5° (zone 2) if too low, until cruise.
  3. 3
    Attitude 0 (zone 5), then wings level (zone 5).
  4. 4
    Attitude +90° (zone 10): vertical climb.
  5. 5
    Roll that places the target in the lift plane (zone 5). Moves to 6 once placed and if the target is lower than me or I am too slow.
  6. 6
    Pull fully until the attitude comes back down to 45° or less: the nose comes back towards the target.
  7. 7
    Attitude towards the target's elevation (zone 10), 0 without a target.
  8. 8
    Wings level (zone 10), then end.

Also ends when the timer goes below 0: 5 s at the start, reset to 10 s at each phase change.

To check in the tool

  • Start in phase 3: level off, then pitch up to 90°, max throttle.
  • Roll in the vertical towards the target, then pull through to 45° of attitude, the target ahead.
  • Each phase must progress in less than 10 s, otherwise the manoeuvre stops.
ID 6 · tag 0x23C

Split-S

  • Trigger tournament
  • Duration 5 s, then 10 s for each phase
  • Throttle cruise (min in phase 2)

Score (base 3)

Zero if: altitude < floor + 4000 m, aircraft with the target on my six, no target, or entity not an aircraft. Then − 2 × uses. Starting phase: 3.

  • Future altitude difference > −2000: −7; ≤ −2000 (target much lower): +2.
  • Same angle and distance terms as ID 5 (+4 / −10, +7 / −2).
  • Indicated speed > cruise: −4 and start in phase 2. Otherwise too slow: +6.
  • My speed > double the target's: −6; > the target's: −3; < the target's: +2.

Sequence

  1. 1
    Climb at +30° (zone 10) up to floor + 2000 m. The score never sets this phase.
  2. 2
    Too fast: attitude +20° (zone 5), throttle towards min speed, until below cruise.
  3. 3
    Attitude 0 (zone 5), then roll to 180° (zone 2): inverted.
  4. 4
    Attitude −90° (zone 10): nose towards the ground.
  5. 5
    Roll that places the target in the lift plane (zone 5).
  6. 6
    Pull fully until the attitude comes back up to −45° or more.
  7. 7
    Attitude towards the target's elevation (zone 10), 0 without a target.
  8. 8
    Wings level (zone 10), then end.

Same timer as ID 5: 5 s at the start, 10 s at each new phase.

To check in the tool

  • Too fast: climb at +20° and throttle back before rolling over.
  • Roll inverted, nose to −90°, roll towards the target, pull through to −45°, then level off facing the target.
  • Never phase 1 from the tournament.
ID 7 · tag 0x228 · fixed node +0xD5

Pursuit

  • Trigger tournament (always a candidate)
  • Duration 1, 2 or 4 s
  • Throttle intercept speed

Score (base 5, clamped to 1…9)

The floor is 1, not 0: even stalled or without a target, ID 7 stays a candidate. The score clears bits 0, 1 and 3 of the node, then:

  • Stalled or no target: 0, raised to 1.
  • FL ≤ 13 and piloting roll (FL, −7) failed: simple computation. +2 if the target is ahead (nose angle < 60) and moving away (aspect < 60), otherwise +2 if d > 1800, −2 otherwise.
  • Full computation: pointing < 90 → bit 0. Nose angle > 110 → bit 0 and −3; nose angle < 50 → +3. Aspect < 90 (moving away) → +4.
  • Target elevation > 45°: −(4 + 6(e−20)/25), and −4 if too slow, otherwise −2. Elevation < −30°: −4. In between: +4.
  • Aircraft and target on my six: stores its relative position at 3 s, bit 1, +10. Otherwise aircraft and head-on: same storage, bit 3, +10.

Setup

Timer 1 s if bit 0, otherwise 2 s; 4 s if bit 1 or 3 and not too low. Bits 2 and 4 cleared.

Sequence (every tick, according to the bits)

  1. b2
    Target on my six (bit 1 and aspect ≥ 80 → bit 2): guidance towards the stored relative position, level, with a 45° climb if I am not too slow; throttle towards the min manoeuvre speed. Bit 2 drops when the aspect goes back below 80.
  2. b4
    Lead point: if the target is within 45° of the nose → intercept, bit 4 cleared. Otherwise guidance towards the stored point; bit 4 cleared once aligned.
  3. b3
    Head-on: when d ≤ 2 × V²/9 (V = target speed) → 4 s timer, bit 4, point = target position + 4 s of its velocity. Otherwise intercept.
  4. —
    No bit: if nose angle ≤ 60 and pointing < 60 → bit 3, then intercept. Otherwise intercept, and end of the manoeuvre if it answers “aligned”.

Intercept (AI_InterceptDispatcher): intercept speed with a 450 m stand-off distance, then aim snap (AI_GunSnapAim_6977, closer than 1800 m, within the window and if the AA roll succeeds), otherwise steering law towards the target.

A collision test (AI_CollisionCourseTest_DD21) runs before the b3 and “no bit” cases. Also ends when the timer expires, or without a target.

Bug in the original: in b4 mode, the absolute position of the lead point is passed to the guidance as a direction.

To check in the tool

  • ID 7 appears in every tournament evaluation with a score of at least 1.
  • Target behind me: 45° climb and speed reduction (bit 2).
  • Head-on: switches to the lead point (bit 4), then back to intercept within 45°.
  • No bit: the manoeuvre ends as soon as it is aligned or the aim snap happens.
ID 13 · tag 0x1B0

Long-range climb

  • Trigger tournament
  • Duration 4 s, counted double while climbing
  • Throttle full, then cruise

Score (base 1)

Zero if: no target, too slow, or altitude ≥ ceiling JDYN+0x86. Then − 2 × uses.

  • If the target is slower than me, at most 400 m below me, my speed ≥ (cruise + min manoeuvre)/2, aircraft, and target on my six or head-on: +5 × (my speed − its speed)/its speed, or +5 if it is flying below min speed.
  • d > 17,700 m and pointing < 30: +5. Otherwise d < 1800: −5, and another −5 if pointing < 20.
  • Agility JDYN+0x8B = a: +(a−1) if a ≥ 2, otherwise −(3−a).
  • Too low: +4.

Sequence

  1. 1
    Target within 17,700 m (horizontal distance): end. Otherwise level guidance towards the target; phase 2 once aligned.
  2. 2
    Too slow: attitude 0 then wings level (zone 5). Otherwise phase 3.
  3. 3
    Zoom climb at 30° + 30° × (speed − cruise)/min speed, at most 60° (30° if I am below cruise), wings level, full throttle. The timer runs down twice as fast. Phase 4 at the end of the timer, if too slow or if the target comes within 17,700 m.
  4. 4
    Attitude 0 (zone 5), cruise throttle, then end.

To check in the tool

  • With a target within 17,700 m, the manoeuvre ends immediately.
  • Otherwise: level turn towards the target, zoom climb between 30° and 60° depending on the excess speed, about 2 s, then levelling-off.
ID 14 · tag 0x19C · fixed node +0xC5

Ground avoidance

  • Trigger reflex, level 4
  • Duration 2 s
  • Throttle full (notch 1 in a fast dive)

Trigger

AI_GroundAvoidReflex_E06C, when no behaviour is running and the reaction level is ≤ 4. Score 10 (otherwise 0) if the autopilot is off and:

  • height above the ground < ground clearance (entity+0xE1) × (1 + sin(roll/2)/2),
  • or altitude below the floor and negative vertical speed.

In the tournament, this node always has a target and its score is 0.

Sequence (every tick)

  1. E
    Ejection test (mode 2): damage > 80 % or stalled.
  2. 1
    If the aircraft is climbing and above the floor: attitude +30°, full throttle, end.
  3. 2
    Otherwise: throttle notch 1 if the nose is below the horizon and the speed above the min manoeuvre speed, otherwise full throttle; attitude +30° (zone 10).

Also ends after 2 s.

To check in the tool

  • Descend below terrain + 200 m (reflexes ticked): the reflex must take over, attitude +30°.
  • Ends as soon as the aircraft climbs above the floor, or after 2 s.
ID 15 · tag 0x188 · fixed node +0xC9

Nose-high and stall recovery

  • Trigger reflex, level 5
  • Duration 2 s
  • Throttle full, idle if stalled with the nose very high

Trigger

AI_StallRecoveryReflex_E159, when no behaviour is running and the reaction level is ≤ 5; it comes before ground avoidance. Score 10 (otherwise 0) if the autopilot is off and the aircraft is stalled, or its indicated speed is ≤ min speed with an attitude > 30°.

Sequence (every tick)

  1. E
    Ejection test (mode 1): damage > 80 %, or stalled below the floor.
  2. 1
    Ends if the nose is at or below the horizon, and the aircraft is no longer both stalled and slow.
  3. 2
    Stalled with an attitude > 60°: throttle at idle (notch 0).
  4. 3
    Otherwise full throttle and, when not stalled, attitude −30° (zone 10).

Also ends after 2 s. The steering law also calls this recovery when the aircraft stalls during guidance.

To check in the tool

  • Pitch the AI aircraft up beyond 30° until min speed: the reflex must push the nose towards −30°, full throttle.
  • Stalled at more than 60° of attitude: throttle at idle.
ID 16 · tag 0x174 · fixed node +0xCD

Speed recovery

  • Trigger tournament
  • Duration 1.5 s
  • Throttle max speed

Score

1 if the FL piloting skill is below 12, otherwise 0. No other condition: it is the fallback manoeuvre of average pilots.

Sequence (every tick)

  1. 1
    Ends if the indicated speed exceeds (cruise + min manoeuvre)/2.
  2. 2
    Otherwise: throttle towards max speed (JDYN+0x80), attitude +5° (zone 5).

Also ends after 1.5 s.

To check in the tool

  • Profile with FL ≥ 12: ID 16 always has a score of 0.
  • Slow aircraft: slight 5° pitch-up, max throttle, stops at the speed threshold or after 1.5 s.

Test protocol with DebugStrike

  1. Open Debug → IA sur l'avion du joueur (AI on the player's aircraft), choose a profile (note its FL and its MVRS weights), enable the AI.
  2. Set up the situation: altitude, speed, and a target picked on the radar in the card's geometry (ahead, behind, head-on, far, lower).
  3. Évaluer le tournoi (evaluate the tournament) once: record the scores. They must match the card (expected zero scores included).
  4. Mode test MVRS (MVRS test mode), “Une fois puis errance” (once then wander), reflexes ticked except for ID 14 and ID 15 alone: start the manoeuvre and record the sequence of phases, attitude, bank, throttle, duration and the reason it ended.
  5. Compare with the card's “To check in the tool” section.
